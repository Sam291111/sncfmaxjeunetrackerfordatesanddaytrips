Quick uses a smaller search budget. Thorough uses a larger bounded budget, with extra room for day-trip searches. Uncapped removes expansion and partial-route caps and may take much longer or use substantial memory.

Uses SNCF’s station- and train-specific minimums, including transfers that are not permitted. If no rule is published for a station pair, the SNCF default is 5 minutes. Leave maximum wait blank to allow long or overnight connections.

Data & caveats

Routes use SNCF’s published GTFS timetable and MAX availability snapshot. MAX availability comes from SNCF’s daily open-data snapshot, so a displayed seat may no longer be bookable. Paid fares are not checked.

Good to know

  • “Lyon (all stations)” includes Part-Dieu, Perrache and Saint-Exupéry TGV; “Paris (all stations)” combines the main Paris terminals.
  • Paid rail connections are considered at your origin or destination; intermediate connections use MAX trains.
  • OUIGO Grande Vitesse trains are schedule candidates only. The public feed has no OUIGO seat counts.
  • Brussels is flagged by the feed but SNCF’s published €0 terms do not list Belgium. Confirm before relying on it.
